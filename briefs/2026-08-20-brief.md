## Rates Brief

- **US 10Y closed 4.647% on Aug 19** (down ~6bp from Aug 18's 4.706%); **30Y closed 5.196%** (down ~9bp from Aug 18's 5.285%, well off Aug 18's 5.33% intraday 19-year high). Curve flattened at the long end on the buyback announcement.
- **Treasury buyback expansion** — Effective Sep 9 through Nov 4: max size of liquidity-support buybacks for 10-to-20Y and 20-to-30Y coupons at least doubles from $2B/op to $4B/op. Bessent's clear signal to compress the long end ahead of AI-related IG supply digestion (~$1.5T YTD) and to counter Warsh's meeting-frequency debate.
- **FOMC July 28-29 minutes released Aug 19 2pm ET** — Hawkish tilt: "policy tightening would likely be necessary if inflation did not decline"; participants judged inflation outlooks "highly uncertain" and skewed to the upside. - **Fed funds target 3.50-3.75%; EFFR ~3.58%.** **CME FedWatch for Sep 15-16 (post-minutes)**: HOLD remains base case; hike odds firmed on the hawkish minutes; cut odds effectively zero heading into Jackson Hole.
